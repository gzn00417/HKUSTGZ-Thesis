\chapter{List of Involved Publications}
\label{ch:publications}

This thesis is based on the following three first-authored publications. Each publication corresponds to one of the three main research chapters of this dissertation, addressing the semantic, observational, and search scaling challenges of video retrieval, respectively.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:universal-video-retrieval}} --- Zhuoning Guo, Mingxin Li, Yanzhao Zhang, Dingkun Long, Pengjun Xie, and Xiaowen Chu. ``Towards Universal Video Retrieval: Generalizing Video Embedding via Synthesized Multimodal Pyramid Curriculum.'' Submitted to \emph{Proceedings of the 34th ACM International Conference on Multimedia (MM '26)}. ACM, 2026.

    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:spatiotemporal-scaling}} --- Zhuoning Guo, Tingyu Song, Mingxin Li, Yanzhao Zhang, Dingkun Long, Pengjun Xie, Xiaowen Chu. ``Demystifying Spatiotemporal Scaling Laws of Multimodal Video Embeddings.'' Submitted to \emph{Proceedings of the European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV 2026)}. Springer, 2026.

    \item \textbf{Chapter~\ref{ch:scale-efficient-retrieval}} --- Zhuoning Guo, Guangxing Chen, Qian Gao, Xiaochao Liao, Jianjia Zheng, Lu Shen, and Hao Liu. ``GPU-accelerated Multi-relational Parallel Graph Retrieval for Web-scale Recommendations.'' In \emph{Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2026 (WWW '26)}. ACM, 2026.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Co-author Permission for Thesis Inclusion}

The publication ``GPU-accelerated Multi-relational Parallel Graph Retrieval for Web-scale Recommendations'' (Chapter~\ref{ch:scale-efficient-retrieval}) is a collaborative work with Prof. Hao Liu from AI Thrust, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). Formal written permission was obtained from Prof. Liu to include this work in this thesis, as required by the university for thesis submission and archival purposes.
