Zhuoning Guo, Mingxin Li, Yanzhao Zhang, Dingkun Long, Pengjun Xie, and Xiaowen Chu. ``Towards Universal Video Retrieval: Generalizing Video Embedding via Synthesized Multimodal Pyramid Curriculum.'' Zhuoning Guo, Tingyu Song, Mingxin Li, Yanzhao Zhang, Dingkun Long, Pengjun Xie, Xiaowen Chu ``Demystifying Spatiotemporal Scaling Laws of Multimodal Video Embeddings.'' Submitted to \emph{Proceedings of the European Conference on Computer Vision (ECCV 2026)}. Springer, 2026.

Zhuoning Guo, Guangxing Chen, Qian Gao, Xiaochao Liao, Jianjia Zheng, Lu Shen, and Hao Liu. ``GPU-accelerated Multi-relational Parallel Graph Retrieval for Web-scale Recommendations.'' In \emph{Proceedings of the ACM Web Conference 2026 (WWW '26)}. ACM, 2026.
